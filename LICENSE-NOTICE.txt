NexusCLI C is an independent, community-made terminal speedcubing timer. It is not an official NexusTimer release and contains no copied NexusTimer source code. The project author reports that RedNaxela, NexusTimer's developer, is supportive of this community effort; that support does not by itself imply official affiliation or endorsement of this specific build.

The program is provided as-is. Back up ~/.nexuscli before making bulk edits or deleting solves.
